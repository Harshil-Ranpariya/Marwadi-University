Vision

To empower learners in Computer Engineering through quality education, advanced technology, innovation, ethical research, and industry-aligned training for global and societal impact.

Mission

1

Deliver quality Computer Engineering education through ICT-enabled pedagogies that foster critical thinking and lifelong learning.

2

Promote innovation, entrepreneurship, and problem-solving through hands-on projects and industry collaboration.

3

Foster ethical research and interdisciplinary learning to address global and societal challenges.

4

Equip students with technical expertise and a global outlook to thrive in dynamic technological environments.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSO)

PSO 1 - Application Development Proficiency

Design, develop, and deploy web and mobile applications using modern tools, engineering practices, and professional standards.

PSO 2 - Research & Emerging Technology Integration

Apply and explore advanced technologies such as AI, IoT, Cloud, and Data Analytics for innovative and research-oriented computing solutions.
